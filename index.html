Day by Day

Ten days, hour by hour.

Six ride stages threaded through gastronomy, history and recovery — sequenced so your body acclimatises before the big days, and timed so your final stage is an official start line.

  1. 01
    Saturday, October 3Arrival

    Bienvenidos a Colombia

    Personal reception at El Dorado International, private transfer minutes away. Your bike case enters the crew's custody the moment you land — our mechanic builds and fine-tunes it overnight, ready before you are. Welcome dinner and ride briefing at 20:30.

    Night in Bogotá · Movich Buró 26
  2. 02
    Sunday, October 4Stage 1 · Prologue34 km · +290 m

    The Legend of El Dorado

    Van to Guatavita, the town of the sacred lagoon where the legend of El Dorado was born, then an opening spin across the savanna toward Sopó. Lunch at Finca de Rigo, Rigoberto Urán's restaurant and a pilgrimage stop for Colombian cyclists. Afternoon in Zipaquirá on foot: the murals honouring Egan Bernal, then the Salt Cathedral carved 180 metres underground.

    Night in Zipaquirá · Camino de la Sal
  3. 03
    Monday, October 5Stage 253 km · +527 m

    The Road to Independence

    From the Puente de Boyacá — where Colombia's independence was sealed in 1819 — on colonial roads to Ráquira, the country's craft capital and town of clay pots. Lunch in Villa de Leyva and the compulsory milhoja at La Galleta. Afternoon: heated pool, sauna and steam, or the Plaza Mayor. Dinner is yours to choose.

    Night in Villa de Leyva · Hotel Getsemaní
  4. 04
    Tuesday, October 6Stage 353.3 km · +759 m

    The Desert & the Dinosaurs

    An ancient seabed turned ochre badlands — the most unexpected landscape of the tour — with a stop at Gondava, the valley of the dinosaurs, and real fossils from when this desert was a sea. Lunch in Sutamarchán: la fritanga, built around Boyacá's finest longaniza. Two hours of thermal springs afterwards.

    Night in Villa de Leyva · Hotel Getsemaní
  5. 05
    Wednesday, October 7Rest day

    The Legends Trail

    No pedalling. Arcabuco and the training climbs that forged Nairo Quintana, then the house where he grew up — photos and onces at the origin point of a legend. Live-fire lunch in Nobsa, ruana country and weaving workshops in Tibasosa. Check-in on the lakeshore at 3,015 metres.

    Night in Lago de Tota · Decameron Rancho Tota
  6. 06
    Thursday, October 8Stage 4 · Queen Stage51.6 km · +1,004 m

    The Queen Stage — Alto del Crucero

    Descend from the lake to the thermal village of Iza, then the great test of the tour: the climb to Alto del Crucero. Lunch at the hotel, then a two-deck catamaran to Isla San Pedro, the largest lake island in Colombia. Playa Blanca in the afternoon — white sand at 3,000 metres. Lakeside dinner built around trout fished from Tota itself.

    Night in Lago de Tota · Decameron Rancho Tota
  7. 07
    Friday, October 9Stage 549.8 km · +506 m

    Lago de Tota, In Full

    Colombia's highest great lake traced in full by bike, between onion fields and blue Andean water, finishing in the plaza of Firavitoba. Then a van over the Alto de Curíes to the Pantano de Vargas — the battle that turned the independence campaign, crowned by Colombia's largest sculpture: 33 metres for the charge of the fourteen lancers. Spa afternoon in Paipa; dinner at the Club Náutico.

    Night in Paipa · Estelar Hotel y Centro de Convenciones
  8. 08
    Saturday, October 10Race eve

    Expo, Monguí & Tejo

    Collect your race kit and official number at the Gran Fondo expo in Duitama. Pueblito Boyacense, then Monguí — one of Colombia's most beautiful pueblos and the cradle of hand-stitched footballs — for a riverside lunch near the Calicanto Bridge and a partial recon of tomorrow's Monguí climb with our riders. Tejo in the afternoon. Carb-loading dinner and an early night.

    Night in Paipa · Estelar Hotel y Centro de Convenciones
  9. 09
    Sunday, October 11Stage 6 · Race Day143.6 km · +2,120 m

    Gran Fondo Boyacá Mundial · 10th Edition

    Race breakfast at 04:30, official start at 06:00 alongside more than 2,000 Colombian riders — the full fondo or the Medio Fondo (103.2 km · +1,403 m), raced or ridden as a celebration. Our crew is on course either way. Lunch in the finish zone, the beer garden with a medal round your neck, and a farewell dinner with music, dancing and awards.

    Night in Paipa · Estelar Hotel y Centro de Convenciones
  10. 10
    Monday, October 12Departure

    Hasta pronto, Boyacá

    Private van to Bogotá at 09:30, bikes packed by our mechanic. We reach El Dorado International at 14:00 — book flights leaving after 17:00. Un abrazo, and see you on the next climb.

    Night in Fin del tour

Before You Ride

Questions, answered.

How do you handle the altitude?

+

The traverse is sequenced to acclimatise you. We open with lower valley stages and build gradually toward the high country around Lago de Tota, so your body adapts before the queen stage. Our guide monitors the group closely, hydration and pacing are managed actively, and the support vehicle is always within reach.

What fitness level do I need?

+

This is a genuine cycling tour for riders comfortable with multi-hour days and sustained climbing — roughly the fitness to ride 3–4 hours back-to-back. You don't need to race; the full support convoy means you can take a lift on any stage and ride within yourself the rest.

Can I bring my own bike, or do you provide one?

+

Both. You're welcome to bring your own — we'll receive, build and maintain it at our workshop. Or rent a professionally fitted carbon road or all-road bike from our own fleet at La Ciclería Café Taller.

What's the weather like in October?

+

High-Andean spring: crisp mornings around 8–12°C, riding hours of 15–20°C, strong sun at altitude and the chance of an afternoon shower. Layers, leg warmers and a rain shell are your friends — a full packing list comes with your confirmation. Boyacá rides year-round, but October is when the Gran Fondo happens, and that is what this departure is built around.

Do I actually get to ride the Gran Fondo?

+

Yes — the traverse is timed so its final stage is an official entry to the Gran Fondo Boyacá Mundial, the 2,000-rider event our founder directs. Choose the full fondo (143.6 km, +2,120 m) or the Medio Fondo (103.2 km, +1,403 m), and ride it as a race or a rolling celebration — with our support crew on course either way.

How small are the groups, really?

+

Ten places maximum, riders and companions combined. It's a deliberate choice — small enough for a high support ratio, real conversation, and lodging in places that can't host a busload.

When should I book my flights?

+

Arrive Saturday, October 3 at any hour — we meet every flight at El Dorado International (BOG). Departure is Monday, October 12: we reach the airport at 14:00, so book flights leaving after 17:00. That covers virtually every overnight direct to Europe and several afternoon departures to North America. Flying earlier? We'll gladly help you arrange an extra night in Bogotá (not included).

What does the cover include — and can I cancel?

+

Your price includes medical and accident assistance for all ten days in Colombia, with road cycling expressly listed rather than excluded as a hazardous sport, as many standard policies do. It does not cover trip cancellation, so we recommend adding your own travel insurance for that. On cancelling: within 5 business days of booking you get everything back, no deductions. After that a published scale applies by proximity to departure, and at any point you may transfer your place to another rider at no cost. If we cancel, you choose a 100% refund or a 110% credit toward a future edition.

Who is this not for?

+

Honestly: riders looking for their first-ever cycling holiday, or for flat spins between cafés. This is real climbing at real altitude — earned, supported, and unforgettable. If you're in doubt, write us; we'll tell you straight.

What if October doesn't work for me?

+

The Founding Departure rides once — at this price, with these ten places. But the Traverse returns, and the 2027 editions are open: 22–31 January, 14–24 May, 20–29 August and 9–18 October (race week again). Tell us which window suits you in the form below and we'll hold your name — 2027 pricing and terms are published closer to the date, and Founding Riders get first refusal.
