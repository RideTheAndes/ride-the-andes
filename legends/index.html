How to enter

Four steps, and you are in

  1. Follow @ridetheandes.co and the partner account for that giveaway.

  2. Like the post.

  3. Comment tagging two cycling friends. Every comment with two different friends counts as one entry — comment as many times as you like.

  4. Bonus: share the post to your story and tag us. That is one extra entry.

All four giveaways close on Tuesday 8 September 2026 at 23:59, Colombia time.

The rules

The fine print, in plain words

  1. Open to entrants aged 18 or over who live in Colombia.

  2. You need a public Instagram account and a real profile. Accounts created for the giveaway do not count, and neither do tags to fake or inactive profiles.

  3. Each comment tagging two different friends counts as one entry. Repeated tags do not add up.

  4. You can enter all four giveaways. The same person can win more than one.

  5. The draw is random and verifiable. It is recorded, and the proof is posted on the account.

  6. The five winners of each giveaway are announced on Wednesday 9 September 2026 on @ridetheandes.co.

  7. Prizes are personal, non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

  8. The Gran Fondo Boyacá Mundial entry is for the 2026 edition and covers the entry only. It does not include travel, accommodation, meals or insurance.

  9. Partner prizes are claimed directly with each partner, on their own terms and opening hours.

  10. We contact winners by direct message from @ridetheandes.co. If there is no reply within 48 hours, the prize is drawn again.

  11. Entrants' data is used only to contact the winners and is not shared with third parties.

  12. Ride The Andes may change or cancel the promotion in the event of force majeure, announcing it on the account.

  13. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Instagram, and is not associated with Instagram.
